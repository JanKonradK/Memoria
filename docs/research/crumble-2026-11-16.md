## Other CookieRun titles considered (rejected)

- CookieRun: Kingdom (2021), CookieRun: OvenBreak (2016): long-running, and neither is called "Crumble".
- CookieRun: OvenSmash (App Store 2026-03-26) and CookieRun Classic (2026-06-24): 2026 Devsisters releases, but neither is called "Crumble".
- CookieRun: New World: announced in March 2026 for console, PC and mobile and still unreleased. Not "Crumble".
- "CookieRun: Crumble Launch Event!" is a cross-promo event that ran inside Kingdom and OvenBreak. It points back to this same game.

Only one Devsisters title carries the word "Crumble", so there is no real ambiguity.

## Preset sources

- **key**: Short and unique. 'cookierun' would collide with Kingdom/OvenBreak if those were ever added.
- **name**: Official store/title name (App Store: 'CookieRun: Crumble - Idle RPG'). See identity.md.
- **color**: Sampled from the current (v1.4+) App Store icon (https://is1-ssl.mzstatic.com/.../512x512bb.png): the dominant flame-orange background is #ff8700 (41.6k px). GingerBrave's dough #e69435 is the largest cluster but reads as a near-duplicate.
- **color2**: The logo's gold lettering, #ffd203 (cookierun.wiki File:Crumble_logo.png). It matches the icon's yellow burst #fed40b.
- **color3**: The logo's chocolate plaque, #3a1709 (the largest cluster in the logo). The icon's ink outline #131c20 is an alternative.
- **titleFont**: The logo uses fat, rounded, slightly bouncing cartoon capitals with a hand-cut edge. Luckiest Guy (Google Fonts) is the closest match; Lilita One is a calmer alternative. Devsisters' own 'CookieRun' font is not on Google Fonts.
- **platform**: iOS, Android, PC via Google Play Games (cookierun.wiki infobox, Notebookcheck).
- **tz**: Every official notice quotes GMT+9: patch notes ('update on October 22 (GMT+9)'), banner countdowns ('November 19 2026 13:00:00 GMT+9') and coupon expiry ('23:59 KST'). The single global release is on one clock (gachago). Asia/Seoul is equivalent; Korea has no DST.
- **dailyResetHour**: cookierun.wiki Crumble Pass page: 'Daily Missions reset every day at midnight (GMT+9)'. Note that the Crumble Dungeon daily ranking settles at 12:00–13:00 KST (patch 1.1) and the Crumb Clash data uses midnight GMT+9 for daily ranking.
- **weeklyResetDay**: NOT VERIFIED. Monday 00:00 KST is assumed, the Devsisters/Korean norm. No source states when Weekly Missions reset. Guild Conquest's battle window closes Monday 12:00 KST, while Arena and Guild Conquest seasons turn over on Thursdays. Check in-game.
- **monthlyResetDay**: Default. No monthly reset is known (the only monthly items found are paid memberships).
- **resources.autoHunt**: 8 hours is the base cap; Sweet Blessing Lv.2 +1h, Gnome Laboratory and packs raise it (PocketGamer resources guide; eog.gg 'Auto Hunt is the whole economy'). Overflow is lost, so the cap is asked for.
- **resources.crumbleDungeonKeys**: 'Up to 3 times daily using Crumble Dungeon Keys' (cookierun.wiki Crumble Dungeon).
- **resources.dailyDungeonKeys**: 'Up to 3 keys refresh daily for each Dungeon, and watching ads earn up to 3 more' (cookierun.wiki Daily Dungeon); the Crumble Pass adds +1 entry; 'tickets refill, so capped tickets are lost' (cookieruncrumbles.com guide). Five dungeons since 1.1.
- **resources.omitted**: Arena Tickets: the regen rate and cap are unknown. A guide says they bank past the display cap, so they are not a loss risk. Collab Dungeon tickets recharge daily but are limited to the event.
- **tasks.daily-missions**: 9 daily missions; 'Log in' and 'Defeat 1,000 Enemies' pay 100 Crystals each, and Mission Points raise the Mission Level, whose milestones are mostly Crystals (cookierun.wiki Missions). Core.
- **tasks.daily-dungeons**: Keys refresh rather than stack, so a skipped day loses 15 runs. The daily mission 'Clear Daily Dungeons 5 times' pays 2 Premium Cookie Cutters, though that counts under Daily Missions.
- **tasks.crumble-dungeon**: 3 keys/day; feeds daily ranking (Innovite, Speed-ups, Pet Tickets) and weekly ranking.
- **tasks.sweet-blessing**: Free once-per-day claim, with rewards by Sweet Blessing level; the PocketGamer guide says it pays Crystals and Blessing Points. Not marked core because the Crystal amount is unconfirmed.
- **tasks.weekly-missions**: Weekly missions pay 500 Crystals ×3 (log in 5 days, Crumble Dungeon ×5, Lucky Roll ×5) + 100 Crystals (Sugar Runes ×30), plus Mission Points (cookierun.wiki Missions). In 1.6 'Shuffle Stellar Links 30 times' became 'Use 30 Auto Hunt Rewards'. Core.
- **tasks.guild-conquest**: Patch 1.2: guild damage is ranked and rewarded weekly and pays Guild Medals for the Guild Medal Exchange (Tea Knight Cookie). The number of daily attempts is unknown.
- **tasks.free-sugarcoating**: Patch 1.2 fixed the shop display of 'when the Weekly Free Sugarcoating Membership is available for subscribing', so there is a free weekly shop claim; Sugarcoating boosts Auto Hunt rewards (patch 1.3). Contents unverified.
- **tasks.omitted**: Lucky Roll (daily free roll for 1–2h of Auto Hunt ×1–×5) is folded into Daily Missions, which require it. The Arena ×3 daily is likewise a Daily Mission. Spending Auto Hunt is noise under the preset rules.
- **processNames**: Mobile game, also on Google Play Games for PC. Its PC process name is not known, so this is left empty.
- **pulls**: cookierun.wiki Gacha (Crumble) and Currencies (Crumble); eog.gg draw simulator (Gacha Level rates; treats the 180-draw pick-up guarantee as reported, not confirmed).

## Pulls

- **premiumCurrency**: Crystals
- **pullCost**: 200
- **multiPull**: 10 pulls = 2,000 Crystals; 30 pulls = 6,000 Crystals
- **petPullCost**: 100
- **tickets**: Premium Cookie Cutter (standard Cookie gacha), Pick-up Cookie Cutter (pick-up banners), Premium / Pick-up Pet Ticket (Pet gacha). TSSR special banners use their own event currency (Flame of Bravery, Light of Knowledge, Wisp of Guidance, Gamer Concentrate), converted to Crystals 1:1 when the banner ends. The collab gacha uses Digital Circus Tickets.
- **pity**: No classic pity counter. Rates depend on a shared account-wide Cookie 'Gacha Level' (1–10) that rises with total Cookie pulls: SSR is impossible below Lv.4, TSSR below Lv.6, and pick-up banners need Gacha Lv.2 (Lv.4 per the 1.1 notes). Pick-up Cookie banners pay draw-count milestone rewards (Crystals). The wiki says a copy of the featured Cookie comes every 180 draws (= 36,000 Crystals); the community also reports 300 for the Pick-up Pet. eog.gg calls this guarantee unconfirmed, and the developer publishes nothing. Each pull also gives Mileage Points for the Mileage Exchange. The collab gacha ignores Gacha Level.
- **freePerUpdateNote**: No community tally of free Crystals per update was found. Known recurring sources: Daily Missions 200 Crystals a day directly, plus Mission Level milestones (100 to 2,500 Crystals each, with cutters and tickets in between); Weekly Missions 1,600 Crystals; quest chains 10–100 each; main-stage boss clears 10 each; every Adventure Note opened pays 100 (82 notes so far, from stages, dungeons and Cookie promotions); Arena season end 400 to 60,000 Crystals by tier; Crumble Dungeon milestones (Crystals only from 50B damage) and weekly top-10 ranking. One-off: launch codes COOKIERUNCRUMBLE1ST and COOKIERUNCRUMBLENO1 at 10,000 Crystals each (expired August 2026).

## Rotating modes

- **Arena season** (14 days): Two-week seasons. Season 6 ran 2026-10-08 16:00 → 10-22 12:00 KST. The client schedules 33 more seasons through January 2028. Season-end rewards: Crystals + Victory Medals by tier; the daily reward is Victory Medals.
- **Guild Conquest** (7 days): Weekly guild boss (Extra-Stuffed Piñata), with groups of 5 guilds each season. Each weekly season starts on a Thursday: the official 9/23 notice says 'the next season starts 9/24'. eog.gg's client calendar shows the battle window running Thu 16:00 → Mon 12:00 KST (07:00 → 03:00 UTC), with results settling on Tuesday. Pays Guild Medals. The boss became Water on 2026-11-05.
- **Rumble Arena**: Cross-server PvP on irregular seasons. Season 1 from 1.4 (2026-09-23), Season 2 2026-10-08 14:00 → 10-21 13:00 KST, Season 3 from 2026-11-05.
- **Dimensional Rift**: Opens after the last main stage. It resets with each major update: S1 2026-09-23, S2 10-08, S4 11-05; S3 presumably 10-22.
- **Crumble Dungeon** (1 days): 3 keys a day. The daily ranking tallies 12:00–13:00 KST; there is also a weekly ranking (Crystals only for the top 10).
- **Daily Dungeons** (1 days): Coin / Cookie EXP / Dough / Innovite / Rune Crystal, 3 keys each a day (+1 with Crumble Pass, +3 from ads); unused keys do not carry over.
