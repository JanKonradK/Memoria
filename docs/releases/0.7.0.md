# Memoria Beta 0.7.0

**Frameless Windows 11 chrome, quiet boot-to-tray & Quick Card pop-out.** The Windows app now draws a frameless title bar whose caption controls stay crisp at any zoom level, boots quietly into the tray (`--background`) while respecting Windows Startup settings, and uses debounced adaptive game watching. Pop out any game into a single-window **Quick Card** from its page, the tray menu, an opt-in global shortcut, or automatically when a watched game closes—update energy, tick dailies, and return cleanly to your full window (restoring maximized or minimized state and preserving open sheet drafts) while staying hidden from screen captures.

**Android Play Store readiness, camera OCR, automatic backups & upgraded widgets.**

- **Camera scanning & on-device OCR:** Point your phone camera at a PC monitor or handheld (or launch from the new **Quick Scan** widget) to read bars, tasks, endgame cycles, and savings completely offline with bundled ML Kit v2. Temporary captures stay in private cache and are deleted right after reading.
- **Automatic folder backups:** Choose a folder once under **Settings → Data** (Storage Access Framework) to keep `memoria-auto-backup.json` updated in the background using safe temporary-file replacement.
- **Upgraded home screen widgets:** Material You surfaces on Android 12+, live countdown timers, a configurable **Single-game** widget (2×2 or 2×1 strip), and a **Quick Scan** widget alongside the refreshed **Glance** widget.
- **28-day reminders & Play build flavour:** Reminders now plan up to 28 days ahead (capped safely below Android's alarm limit), with a dedicated Play-compliant build flavour (`play`) alongside the self-updating GitHub build (`direct`), 16 KB memory-page alignment verification, scoped HoYoLAB cookies, and Pro billing groundwork.

**Smarter HUD-aware OCR & long-term data integrity.** Multi-pass Windows OCR targets top HUD bands and merges spatial word-box matches with confidence labels. Deleted tombstones slim down after 90 days (365-day retention) while keeping Activity undo restores intact, and energy history thins evenly above 20,000 readings while preserving each resource's latest values.

**Refined layout, motion, Settings & daily streaks.** Consolidated Settings into 8 focused sections, unified buttons and segmented controls with pointer-accurate touch targets, refined View Transition logo morphs and sheet handoffs, added daily streak tracking under **Settings → Activity & About**, and replaced publisher livestream key art with clean typographic version headers.
