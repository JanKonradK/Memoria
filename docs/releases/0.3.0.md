# Memoria Beta 0.3.0

**Update every device before syncing.** This version stores game data in a newer format, so the PC and the phone must both run 0.3.0 before sync works again. An older copy refuses to sync instead of losing anything. Your data is kept.

**A new home for your games.** Every game now has its own place in a rail down the side (a dock along the bottom on a phone), with its logo, how long until it next needs you, and two thin meters for its stamina and its reserve. Pick a game and its page holds everything about it: tracking in three columns, its events, and its own Pulls, Quests and News tabs. Start a game from Memoria and the app follows you to it.

**Today is one list.** Everything worth doing across your games, in the order it matters: stamina about to cap, dailies, weeklies, events ending soon. A strip shows the day ahead and a column what is coming up. Each stamina step shows its reserve under it, and tells you when a full bar is spilling into the reserve rather than going to waste.

**Stamina and its reserve, together.** A bar and what banks its overflow (Reserved Trailblaze Power, Condensed Resin, Waveplate Crystals) are now one control on Today, the game page and the rail, instead of two separate rows.

**Calendar, three ways.** An agenda by day, a month grid that shows how many more events each day holds, and an ending-soon board. The timeline packs events into lanes, filters by game logo, and marks now, maintenance and countdowns on the bars themselves.

- **Startup:** a short intro covers the app while it loads, so it opens on a finished screen. Tap to skip it once the app is ready.
- **Calendar events** you add go straight in, and livestreams live under **Catch up**.
- **CookieRun: Crumble:** the 19 November update, from the official patch notes.
- Smoother on battery: one shared clock drives every countdown, and nothing ticks while Memoria is in the background.

**Also in this version** (0.2.1 and 0.2.2 were not published on their own):

- **Real alarms on Android.** A reminder set to Alarm rings like a clock app: on time even in Doze, over the lock screen, until you snooze or dismiss it, and again after a restart. **Settings → Reminders → No-alarm times** keeps alarms quiet at work or while you sleep. To ring over the lock screen, allow it once from **Settings → Reminders → Alarms over the lock screen**.
- **Sync away from home.** A paired phone can reach your PC from anywhere through a small relay you deploy to your own Cloudflare account (see `relay/README.md`, then paste its address in **Settings → Device sync**). Everything stays end-to-end encrypted.
- **Windows updates install again.** If an earlier update failed for you, this one installs normally from **Settings → Updates**; problems are logged to `%APPDATA%\memoria\updates\install.log`.
- **CookieRun: Crumble** is a preset, with its Thursday resets. **Neverness to Everness:** City Stamina has no reserve, and Europe stays on UTC+1 all year.
- **Quests:** complete story and quest catalogs for every preset game. **Pulls → Version history:** the free currency of every version since launch.
- **Accessibility:** every button's spoken name includes the words it shows, so voice control finds it by what is on screen.
- Updated to the newest compatible React, Vite, Tailwind, Capacitor, Tauri and Rust crates, on Node 26 LTS and Rust 2024.
