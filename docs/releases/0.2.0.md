# Memoria Beta 0.2.0

**Update every device before syncing.** This version stores quest progress, so the PC and the phone must both run 0.2.0 before Wi-Fi sync works again. An older copy refuses to sync instead of losing anything. Your data is kept.

**Reminders and alarms**

- Choose how each kind of reminder arrives in Settings → Reminders: **Silent**, **Gentle**, **Standard** or **Alarm**. Dailies, weeklies, events, energy caps, your own reminders and the evening digest can each have their own.
- Alarm-style reminders ring with a ring you pick (Classic, Pulse, Chime or Ring) and offer **Snooze 10 min**. On Windows they stay on screen and ring until answered; on Android they ring at alarm volume.
- A single reminder can ring as an alarm on its own: pick it under **Arrives as**.
- **Test** beside each style sends a sample right away, so you hear what you chose.
- Today now asks, once, when something keeps reminders from reaching you: reminders switched off, notifications blocked by Android or Windows, exact timing not allowed, or battery saving holding them back. Each opens the right settings page.

**Quests**

- A new **Quests** tab: a companion for each game's story. Main-story acts, world-quest series and character stories, each with the parts you tick off, partly or fully, the characters in them, how to start them and a wiki link.
- **Continue** and **Ready to start** show where you left off and what is unlocked next. Search finds quests by name, place or character; tap a character to see their other quests.
- The game's events running now sit at the top, with their parts.
- Add your own quest lines for anything the list is missing. Progress is per account and syncs between devices.
- The lists come from community wikis and are a start, not complete: Genshin, Honkai: Star Rail, Zenless Zone Zero, Wuthering Waves, Neverness to Everness and Arknights: Endfield.

**Pulls**

- Version history now starts at each game's first version, and adds Neverness to Everness and Arknights: Endfield.
- Every version lists the limited banners of each phase.
- Totals count free currency only: no monthly pass, no paid battle pass, no top-ups. Free limited pull tickets count at the cost of one pull.
- Totals were re-checked against their sources. Figures that could not be traced were replaced by sourced estimates or marked as such, and each version's notes say which.
