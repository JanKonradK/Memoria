# Memoria Beta 0.4.0.2

**First run**
- Setup comes in two steps: pick your games, then set each one's server (EU, NA or Asia), nickname and current energy. The server starts on the region nearest your timezone.
- The tour runs after setup, over your real dashboard. It now covers Pulls, and right-click to mark an event done.
- On the phone, setup offers **Connect to PC** first, so your games come over instead of being added twice.

**Pulls**
- One game at a time: pick it at the top. Its savings and pity sit side by side. Version history is the last choice.
- No more Start saving step. Type your balance and save.
- **Passes you buy:** turn on the monthly pass and the battle pass for each game. Each shows what it adds a day, and the income line splits free and paid.

**Settings**
- Change each game's nickname and server right in the Games list. Edit… opens everything else.
- Home timezone and sleep window move to a new General section.

**Fixes and changes**
- Right-click an event on the timeline to mark it done.
- Mark done closes the event popup.
- The PC no longer offers Connect to PC.
- Complete dailies is now a small Tick all button on the Daily row. Undo replaces it after you use it.
