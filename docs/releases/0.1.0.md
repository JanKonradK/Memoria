# Memoria Beta 0.1.0

Version numbers start again at 0.1.0, and the number you see is the real one. Copies on 4.0.3 or earlier do not offer this release as an update: install it once by hand (below). Your data is kept.

- **Windows:** run **Memoria-setup-x64.exe**. It installs over your current copy in the same folder, keeps your data, and from then on Memoria updates itself again.
- **Android:** open **Memoria-android.apk** and install it over the existing app. Do not uninstall first; that would delete the phone's data.

**Install and updates**

- A Windows installer: no administrator prompt, Start menu and Desktop shortcuts, and an uninstaller in Settings → Apps that keeps your data. The ZIP is still there for a portable copy.
- Patch releases no longer take the whole screen; a dot on Settings says one is waiting.
- Android updates can come through Obtainium; the README says how.

**Colours**

- Choose a colour palette in Settings → General: Memoria, Catppuccin, Kanagawa, Rosé Pine, Gruvbox or Tokyo Night, with their dark flavours and light variants, and an accent within it. Every combination stays readable.
- The theme follows the device's light or dark setting unless you choose one.

**Reminders**

- Reminders that fire: dailies an hour before reset, weeklies, events, and energy caps, with exact alarms on Android and a reminder step in first-run setup.
- A Reminders section in Settings with quiet hours (off by default; they move reminders, never drop them), an evening digest and your own repeating reminders.
- Pass tracker: a reminder before the monthly pass and the battle pass run out.

**Today and games**

- **All clear:** when every daily is done and nothing caps before the next reset, Today says so and names the next thing due.
- Faster entry: Spent all on each energy bar, quick spends, tick a game's dailies from its ring, and reserve steps of 10.
- After a game closes on the PC, Memoria offers to record its energy and dailies.
- Codes from the feed on Today, with Copy, Redeem and Redeemed. Check-in tasks open their page.
- Pull planning shows the chance of getting the featured 5-star by the banner's end.
- Three short hints after setup replace the tour.

**Quieter screens**

- The app bar shows Refresh and sync status only when something needs attention.
- Times, dates and counts are set upright, not in light italic.
- The Timeline says nothing about the calendar feed while it is current.
- A tab slides in from the side it came from, and a swipe follows your finger.

**Phone sync and security**

- Memoria no longer changes a Windows network from Public to Private; Settings explains how to do it yourself.
- Sync keys are protected by Windows and the Android Keystore.

The one-file `Memoria.html` download is retired: it could not sync or update itself.
