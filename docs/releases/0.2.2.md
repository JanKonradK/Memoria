# Memoria Beta 0.2.2

**Real alarms on Android.** A reminder set to Alarm now rings like a clock app: on time even in Doze, over the lock screen, until you snooze or dismiss it, and it is set again after a restart. **Settings → Reminders → No-alarm times** keeps alarms quiet at work or while you sleep: an alarm that falls in one arrives silently instead. To ring over the lock screen, allow it once from **Settings → Reminders → Alarms over the lock screen**.

**Sync away from home.** A paired phone can reach your PC from anywhere through a small relay you deploy to your own Cloudflare account (see `relay/README.md`, then paste its address in **Settings → Device sync**). Everything stays end-to-end encrypted; the relay only passes sealed packets. On your Wi-Fi the phone still talks to the PC directly.

- **CookieRun: Crumble** is a preset, with its Thursday resets.
- **Neverness to Everness:** City Stamina has no reserve, and Europe stays on UTC+1 all year. Existing games are corrected.
- **Quests:** complete story and quest catalogs for every preset game from launch.
- **Pulls → Version history:** the free currency of every version since launch, for every preset game, with each phase's banners and sources.
- **Design:** clearer game headings, history chart labels that never overlap, timeline labels that stay readable, a tidier first run, readable monograms on small custom-game logos, a Settings game list that no longer breaks names on phones, and a soft edge wherever a list scrolls out of view.
- **Accessibility:** every button's spoken name now includes the words it shows, so voice control finds it by what is on screen.
- Updated to the newest compatible React, Vite, Tailwind, Capacitor, Tauri and Rust crates, on Node 26 LTS and Rust 2024.
