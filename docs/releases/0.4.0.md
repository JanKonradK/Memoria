# Memoria Beta 0.4.0

**Eight looks.** Choose how Memoria draws itself in **Settings → Appearance**: Classic, Instrument, Soft HUD, Clean, Glass, Arcade, Paper or Neon. Each works with every colour palette, in dark and light, and reaches Today, the game pages, the dock and the game logos. First-time setup offers the same choice.

**A floating dock.** On a phone or an upright tablet the navigation now sits at the bottom, under your thumb: Calendar, Games, Today, Notes and Settings, with Today in the middle. **Games** raises a row of your game logos with their countdowns. On a PC the same places run down the left, with your games listed under them.

**Notes.** Every game's notes on one page, with search, and a Notes tab on each game.

**Swipe and drag.** On a phone or tablet:

- **Swipe a row right to complete it and left to take it back.** Works on Today, a game's tasks and events, the Calendar's agenda and Soon views, and quests.
- **Drag along an energy bar** (Original Resin and the like) to set the reading, with a small tick at every step.
- **Swipe a game's page** through its tabs and on to the next game.
- Short vibrations confirm each one. Turn them off in **Settings → Appearance → Vibration**.

**Minimal.** **Settings → Appearance → Detail → Minimal** shows the facts and leaves out the explanations: hints, subtitles and labels go, and "Updated 13 min ago" becomes a sync mark and "13m".

- **Accounts:** if you play the same game on more than one server or under more than one nickname, its page shows those accounts as a sideways strip.
- **Sized to the screen:** on a tablet everything is drawn larger to fill it; a small phone draws a touch smaller.
- **Logos:** small game logos show the game's short name (ZZZ, NTE, WuWa) instead of a single letter.
- On a touch screen, tapping a game no longer flashes a tooltip.
- Rounder corners throughout.
