# Memoria Beta 0.4

- New **Pulls** tab with three views:
  - **Savings:** enter your currency and tickets, then pick a banner or a date. It shows how many pulls to expect by then, as a range from recent versions' free income.
  - **Pity:** tap in pulls as you make them and record each 5★. It shows your pity, whether the next 5★ is guaranteed, how many pulls until the featured one, and where past 5★s landed. A banner you save for uses your pity as its target. Covers Genshin, Star Rail, Zenless Zone Zero and Wuthering Waves.
  - **Version history:** how many free pulls each version gave, compared across games, with sources.
- **Reminders on Windows:** the PC can show the same energy and deadline alerts as the phone, even from the tray. Turn on **Reminders on this PC** in the tray menu or Settings. It is off by default so the phone and PC don't both alert.
- Game reset times on the PC now follow daylight saving.
- Phone sync through Windows is allowed only on private networks and Tailscale, no longer on public Wi-Fi.
- Fixes: a game with an unknown timezone no longer stops the app from opening, sync no longer fails on some saved data, non-Latin game process names no longer crash the PC app, and one device can't block others from pairing.

Update the PC and the phone together. A 0.3 device won't sync with 0.4 data.

If you allowed phone sync through Windows before, right-click the Memoria tray icon and select **Allow phone sync through Windows** again. This replaces the old rule.
