# Memoria Beta 0.3.3

- The Windows app is rebuilt in Rust. The download is much smaller, it starts faster, and it stays in the tray.
- Phone sync works away from home through Tailscale. Install it on the PC and phone with the same account. After the next sync at home, the phone reaches the PC from any network.
- The phone remembers every PC address, so sync keeps working when the PC's address changes.
- A game's page is tidier. Edit and Manage events are one Edit menu at the top. The dailies ring sits beside a larger title. Each currency's name, round − and + buttons and value share one line, and values line up.
- Every event, in every game, is simply not started, partly done or done. Tap its tick to step through. Memoria no longer guesses how many parts an event has.
- Android home-screen widget with your most urgent games, their energy and daily progress.
- Genshin 7.2 "Hour of Ruin": the Mitya and Furina banners, the weapon banner, the Archon Quest, exploration and event rewards, the Battle Pass, Miliastra events, and the November and December Spiral Abyss and Theater.
- Livestreams has recaps for Star Rail 4.7, NTE 1.5 and Genshin 7.2. Wuthering Waves 3.8 airs November 6 at 11:00 UTC.

If you allowed phone sync through Windows before, right-click the Memoria tray icon and select **Allow phone sync through Windows** again. This lets Tailscale connections in.
